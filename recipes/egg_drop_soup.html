Egg Drop Soup

This recipe features fresh chives and a touch of ginger for easy, authentic Chinese flavors.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups of chicken broth, divided
  • 2 tablespoons of chopped fresh chives
  • 1/4 teaspoons of salt
  • 1/8 teaspoons of ground ginger
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons of cornstarch
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 egg yolk
